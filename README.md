## 🛠️ About Me

A couple of years ago (around 2025), I made the deliberate decision to completely wipe Windows and dive headfirst into Linux. After racking up plenty of system-breaking "bumps and bruises" along the way (and occasionally crashing Debian on a weekly basis), I adapted and turned local environment recovery into a superpower. I love adapting and tweaking operating systems to fit specific engineering tasks—typically cycling through distributions (Linux Mint, Ubuntu Studio, EndeavourOS, Kali) on a monthly basis.

### 🎯 Primary Technical Vectors

* **DevOps & Linux Infrastructure:** Born out of necessity from daily Linux administration, containerization, and automation workflows.
* **Network Infrastructure & Tunneling:** Bypassing strict routing barriers and managing advanced multi-tunnel architectures (`iptables`, `iproute2`, AmneziaWG, Xray VLESS, GoST).
* **Python Development:** Learning and expanding core programming skills at my own pace (structured around algorithmic paths like Generation Python).
* **Music Information Retrieval (MIR) & Audio Informatics:** Bridging my formal classical music education with software engineering, sound synthesis, DSP analysis, and automated media pipelines.
